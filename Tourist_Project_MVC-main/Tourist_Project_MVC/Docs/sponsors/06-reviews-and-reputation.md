# Reviews & Reputation

Tourists can leave ratings and text reviews after redeeming rewards or visiting branches. **SponsorReviewController** surfaces this feedback for you to monitor and respond to.

## Reading reviews

- The **Reviews** page lists every review sorted by most recent.
- Filters let you view only positive, neutral, or negative feedback.
- Click a review to see the full text and the tourist’s profile context.

## Reputation impact

- Your average rating is calculated from all reviews.
- Higher-rated sponsors appear more prominently in **Near Me** and explore pages.
- Consistently low ratings can affect trust and redemption volume.

## Responding to feedback

- Use the review detail page to add an internal note or a public reply.
- Public replies demonstrate responsiveness and can improve a tourist’s perception of the brand.
- For offensive or inaccurate reviews, contact support through **Support & Approval** to request moderation.

> **Tip:** Thank tourists for positive reviews and address concerns in negative ones. A visible, courteous response can convert a frustrated tourist into a loyal repeat visitor.
