# Support & Approval

This section covers two sponsor-specific flows: the initial Admin approval request and ongoing support ticket management via **SponsorNotificationController**.

## Account approval

1. After registering as a sponsor, your account status is **Pending Approval**.
2. An Admin reviews your submission and either approves or rejects it.
3. You are notified through the site notification system when a decision is made.
4. Once approved, the full Sponsor Portal becomes accessible.

> **Note:** You can browse public pages while waiting, but portal features remain hidden until approval.

## Support tickets

- Open a support ticket for issues related to branches, rewards, redemptions, or account access.
- Attach files or screenshots to help the support team reproduce the problem.
- Track replies in your ticket history; Admins and support staff update tickets as they work toward resolution.

## Responding to Admin requests

- If an Admin requests additional information for approval, submit the required documents promptly.
- Delayed responses can extend the approval timeline.

> **Tip:** Keep your registration details and business documents up to date to avoid unnecessary back-and-forth during approval.
