# Sponsor Dashboard

The **Sponsor Portal** dashboard is your command center for tracking performance across branches, rewards, and tourist interactions. It is powered by **SponsorPortalController**.

## Dashboard overview

- **Stat cards** summarize total redemptions, active rewards, branch count, and average rating.
- **Charts** show redemption trends over time so you can adjust reward availability.
- **Recent activity** highlights new redemptions and review submissions.

## Reports

- Export redemption history and reward performance data.
- Filter by branch, date range, or reward type to focus on specific periods.
- Reports are generated server-side and downloaded as files.

### Navigating the portal

- Use the left sidebar or top navbar to move between Portal, Branches, Rewards, Reports, and Docs.
- Links with notification badges indicate pending items such as new redemptions or support tickets.

> **Note:** Dashboard data refreshes when you reload the page. Real-time updates are not yet supported in this version.

## Staying on top of support

- The notification bell in the navbar surfaces items that need action, such as redemption approvals or support ticket replies.
- Click a notification to jump directly to the relevant section.
