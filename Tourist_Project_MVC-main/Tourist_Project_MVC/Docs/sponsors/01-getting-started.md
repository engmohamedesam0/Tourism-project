# Getting Started

Welcome to the EGYXPLORE sponsor experience. This guide covers registration, admin approval, and your first login to the sponsor portal.

## Registering as a sponsor

1. Use **Sign Up** and select the **Sponsor** role.
2. Fill in your business name, contact email, and primary location.
3. Submit the registration form.

> **Note:** Your account remains in a pending state until an Admin reviews and approves it. You will receive a notification once approval is complete.

## First login

After approval:

- Sign in normally from the top-right **Sign In** button.
- The **Sponsor Portal** link appears in your primary navbar, alongside Branches, Rewards, Reports, and Docs.
- Open the portal to complete your sponsor profile and start managing locations.

> **Tip:** Have your business license or registration details ready during registration to speed up the Admin approval process.
