# Your Profile

**TouristProfileController** gives you full control over your public identity on EGYXPLORE. Keeping your profile current helps sponsors recognize you and improves personalization.

## Editing details

- Update your name, nationality, and short bio so the community knows who you are.
- Save changes and they reflect immediately across the platform.

## Profile picture

- Upload a photo from your device.
- Recommended size: at least 200 × 200 pixels.
- The picture appears in reviews, support tickets, and your public profile page.

> **Note:** Uploaded images are stored on the server. Profile pictures are visible to Admins and Sponsors for verification purposes.

## Preferences

- Set your default location to improve **Near Me** accuracy.
- Turn notifications on or off for reward drops, mission updates, and support replies.
- Link preferences are used only to tailor recommendation rankings inside Explore.

> **Tip:** A complete profile with a photo and location set increases trust from sponsors and improves your chances of receiving personalized reward offers.
