# Getting Started

Welcome to **EGYXPLORE** — your smart gateway to Egypt’s most iconic destinations, hidden gems, and local sponsor perks. This guide walks you through everything you need to begin exploring as a tourist.

## Creating an account

To unlock personalized features like trip planning, rewards, and the AI chat assistant, you’ll need a tourist account:

1. Click **Sign Up** in the top-right corner of the site.
2. Choose the **Tourist** role and fill in your details (name, email, password).
3. Agree to the terms and submit the form.
4. Once registered, use **Sign In** to access your dashboard.

> **Note:** If you sign up as a Sponsor instead, an Admin must approve your account before the sponsor portal becomes available.

## Your first exploration

After logging in, your navbar adjusts to include tourist-specific links:

- **Explore** — browse all registered destinations with photos, descriptions, and map positions.
- **Trip** — plan and save multi-stop itineraries.
- **Near Me** — discover sponsors and destinations sorted by physical proximity.
- **Rewards** — view and redeem real-world perks from sponsors.

## Quick orientation tips

- Use the **language switcher** in the top utility bar to toggle between English, Arabic, and Spanish.
- The **AI Assistant** button (bottom-right) can answer questions about destinations, help build trips, and explain platform features.
- Bookmark destinations by visiting their detail pages; bookmarked sites appear in your profile for quick reference later.

> **Tip:** Complete your profile photo and location preferences early — the platform uses these to personalize recommendations and calculate accurate “Near Me” distances.
