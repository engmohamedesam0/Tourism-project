# Reviews & Support

Tourists can leave reviews and raise support requests at any time. This guide explains how to monitor reviews and handle the support workflow from your admin console.

## What it is

- **Reviews** — ratings and feedback tourists leave on destinations and sponsor experiences. They feed the average ratings shown across the platform.
- **Support tickets** — structured requests raised by tourists or sponsors (account help, technical issues, sponsor-related questions). Every ticket flows into your **Support Inbox**.

## Why use it

- Catch problems early — a spike of negative reviews or open tickets tells you something needs attention.
- Resolve requests quickly so tourists and sponsors stay happy.
- Keep the platform's reputation data healthy and accurate.

## Viewing tourist reviews

1. Review data is aggregated across the platform — the average rating appears in the platform statistics and on the **Admin Dashboard**.
2. Individual reviews are linked to the destination or sponsor they refer to; open a destination's details to see its rating and feedback.
3. Sponsors can also respond to reviews through their own portal — you can follow up with them from the **Sponsors** area if feedback is going unanswered.

> **Note:** Reviews are written by tourists and displayed automatically. Keep an eye on aggregate ratings and address patterns of negative feedback through the support workflow.

## Working the support inbox

1. Open the **Sponsors** menu and click **Support Inbox** (or go to `/AdminSupport/Index`).
2. The list shows every ticket — from tourists, sponsors, or tourists routed to sponsors — with status (Open, In Progress, Resolved).
3. Use the **status**, **category**, and **submitter** filters, plus **search**, to find a specific request.
4. A badge on the Support Inbox link shows how many tickets still need attention.

## Responding to a support request

1. Click a ticket to open its details — you'll see the submitter, category, description, and any response already provided.
2. Write your **response** in the reply box.
3. Submit the response. The submitter is notified, and the ticket's status moves through the workflow (Open → In Progress → Resolved).
4. Mark the ticket **Resolved** once the issue is closed.

## Following up on reported issues

- Reopen the ticket at any time from the **Support Inbox** if the issue persists.
- Check the resolved-this-month statistic on the inbox overview to gauge how quickly requests are being handled.
- If an issue involves a sponsor, use the ticket details to see the sponsor's response alongside yours, and follow up with them directly if needed.

> **Tip:** Resolve tickets as soon as they arrive — the notification bell and inbox badge will keep prompting you until they are closed.
