# Managing Sponsors

Sponsors are businesses that offer rewards and perks to tourists. As an Admin you control the sponsor registry, approve new sponsors, and keep their information and content accurate.

## What it is

The sponsor area shows every business partner on the platform — their details, branches, rewards, and approval status — together with the approval queue for new sponsor registrations.

## Why use it

- Approve or reject new sponsor accounts before they can operate.
- Keep business information (names, types, cities, contacts) correct.
- Monitor what sponsors are publishing — rewards and offers — and how they are performing.

## Viewing sponsors

1. Open the **Sponsors** page from the admin menu (or go to `/Sponsor/Index`).
2. The overview shows the total sponsor count, pending approvals, active rewards, and total redemptions.
3. Use **search** and the **type** filter to find a specific business.
4. Click a sponsor's name to open their **details page**, which shows their profile and the rewards they offer.

## Managing sponsor information

1. On the **Sponsors** page, click **Edit** on the sponsor you want to update.
2. Change the **name**, **type**, **city**, or **contact information**.
3. Save — the updated details appear immediately on the sponsor's public profile and in their portal.

> **Note:** Sponsors manage their own branches, rewards, and responses through their portal (see the For Sponsors documentation). Admin edits are for registry-level information.

## Approving or rejecting new sponsors

1. Open the **Sponsors** menu and click **Approvals** (or go to `/SponsorApproval/Index`).
2. Review each pending request — the sponsor's business details and contact information.
3. Click **Approve** to activate the sponsor's account, or **Reject** to decline it.
4. The sponsor is notified of the decision. Once approved, the sponsor can sign in and use their portal.

> **Note:** A badge on the Approvals link shows how many requests are waiting for your review.

## Managing sponsor content, rewards, and offers

- **Rewards and offers** are created against a sponsor from the **Rewards** page — choose the sponsor when creating a reward so it appears in their catalog.
- If a sponsor's rewards are wrong or inactive, edit their status (**Active**, **Paused**, **Removed**) from the **Rewards** page.
- **Branches** are managed by the sponsor themselves from their portal; you can see them on the sponsor's details page.

## Reviewing sponsor activity

- **Redemptions** — the count of rewards tourists actually redeemed appears on the **Sponsors** overview and in the platform statistics.
- **Support tickets** — tickets raised by or about a sponsor land in your **Support Inbox**.
- **Reviews** — tourist feedback on sponsor experiences feeds the ratings shown on sponsor and destination pages.

> **Tip:** Run a platform overview (or ask the AI assistant for platform statistics) to see how sponsors are performing across the whole platform at once.
