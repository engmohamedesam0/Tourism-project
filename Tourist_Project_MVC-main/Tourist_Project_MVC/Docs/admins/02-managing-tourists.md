# Managing Tourists

Every tourist account on EGYXPLORE is visible to you through the **Tourists** area. This guide explains how to view tourists, inspect their profiles, update their information, and monitor what they are doing on the platform.

## What it is

The tourist management pages give you a complete registry of everyone who registered as a tourist, together with their profile details and platform activity.

## Why use it

- Answer support questions faster by looking up a tourist's account.
- Keep profile data accurate (names, emails, nationalities).
- Understand how engaged tourists are with missions, trips, and rewards.

## Viewing tourists

1. Sign in with your **Admin** account.
2. Open the **Tourists** page from the admin menu (or go to `/Tourist/Index`).
3. Browse the table showing every tourist with their name, email, and nationality.
4. Use the **search box** to find a tourist by name, email, or nationality.
5. Use the **nationality filter** to narrow the list to a specific country.

## Inspecting a tourist profile

1. On the **Tourists** page, click the tourist's name or the **Details** button.
2. The profile page shows their personal information (name, email, nationality, contact details).
3. Review their platform footprint — saved trips, favorite destinations, mission progress, and rewards history are linked to the profile.

## Managing or updating tourist information

1. On the **Tourists** page, click **Edit** next to the tourist you want to update.
2. Change the relevant fields (name, nationality, contact details, and so on).
3. Click **Save** to apply the changes.
4. The updated information immediately appears in the tourist's profile and anywhere it is displayed (for example, in support tickets).

> **Note:** Password and login management for an account is handled from the **Accounts** page, not from the tourist profile. Deleting an account from **Accounts** preserves the tourist's historical data but removes their ability to sign in.

## Monitoring tourist activity

- **Missions and points** — the gamification system tracks every completed mission and the points earned; you can see progress through the tourist's profile and the platform statistics.
- **Favorites and trip plans** — tourists save destinations and build itineraries; these are visible through their profile.
- **Support tickets and reviews** — when a tourist reports an issue or leaves a review, it appears in your **Support Inbox** and in the platform's review data.

> **Tip:** Use the platform statistics or ask the AI assistant for a platform overview to see aggregate tourist counts and activity without opening individual profiles.
